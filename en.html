Jiam ("we", "us") operates the mobile game Riftsworn (Korean title 리프트스원: 균열의 서약자, package com.seolasoft.riftsworn, the "Game"). This policy explains what information the Game processes and why, how long it is kept, and how you can control it.

Summary

The Game is an offline single-player game with no sign-up or login, and we do not ourselves collect personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number. Your game progress is stored only on your device and is never sent to our servers. The only ads are rewarded ads that appear only when you choose to watch one; there are no banner or interstitial ads. Google AdMob processes the data below to show these ads. The current version has no in-app purchases and does not use any separate analytics or crash-reporting service.

4. International transfers

Ad data may be processed in the United States and other countries where Google operates, transmitted over encrypted (TLS) connections when the ad SDK starts and when ads are loaded or shown. Transfers out of the EEA, UK, and Switzerland rely on lawful transfer mechanisms adopted by Google, such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

5. Advertising and your choices

The Game shows rewarded ads only, and only when you choose to watch one: to double the coins earned on a journey (run), offered on the result screen, or to revive once per journey after losing a battle. There are no banner or interstitial ads. Personalized ads are shown only if you consent or allow them on your device. In regions where consent is required (such as the EEA, UK, and Switzerland), you can make your choice in the consent message and change it at any time in the Game under Settings → Privacy options, which appears when consent applies to you. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in device Settings → Privacy (or Google) → Ads. If you opt out, you can still play the Game as usual; ads will not be personalized. The Game itself does not use cookies.

6. Deletion and your rights

  • Game data — there is no in-game delete screen. Clear the app's data in device Settings → Apps → Riftsworn → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app. All game data on the device is then removed.
  • Ad consent — change it in the Game under Settings → Privacy options (shown when consent applies).
  • Advertising ID — reset or delete it in device Settings → Privacy (or Google) → Ads.
  • Other requests — email us at the address below and we will reply within 10 days. We do not keep game data on any server, so data on your device can be removed with the steps above.

See Data deletion for step-by-step instructions.

7. Security

  • All ad traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Game progress is stored only in the app's private storage on your device; we do not operate a game server.
  • Access to the ad and store management consoles is limited to the operator, and we process only the minimum data needed.

8. Children

The Game is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (under 14 in Korea, or the applicable age in your country). If we learn that we have, we will delete it promptly.

9. EEA, UK, and Switzerland

Jiam is the controller. Our legal bases are: consent for ad storage and personalized ads (collected through the consent message); and our legitimate interests in security, fraud prevention, and serving non-personalized ads. You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, port, and object to processing, to withdraw consent at any time (Settings → Privacy options), and to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.
